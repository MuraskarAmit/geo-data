# Attribution

The country maps in `packs/` are made by Make Me Smarter's data build from the sources below. Each source's data stays under its own licence and terms, listed with it, and each credit line is the one the app's Sources page and the website show. Make Me Smarter's own parts are under the terms in `LICENSE`.

India's boundaries are drawn according to India's official position, simplified for small-scale maps (data: Natural Earth, India view). These maps are for learning, not navigation, and they are not an authoritative statement on any boundary.
